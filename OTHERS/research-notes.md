# Affiliate offer decision — 4 October 2026

This is an acquisition hypothesis and private planning analysis, not a promise of earnings. No campaign conversion data, audience size or ad budget was supplied. Initial target: English-speaking freelancers and small agencies with an actual CRM need. A subscribed client means a distinct, active, paying HighLevel agency account attributed to you, not a lead, trial, workshop attendee or a sub-account belonging to an agency.

## Recommendation

Use the bootcamp as the first onboarding campaign. Its live implementation support gives a beginner a reason to activate the software. Use pricing for buyers already choosing a plan, and Pro only for agencies with a demonstrated SaaS requirement. This recommendation predicts retained paid customers per qualified visitor over 60–90 days, not the shortest possible time to the first charge. Pricing and the ordinary 14-day trial can bill sooner than the bootcamp's 30-day trial. No public evidence establishes which of these links converts fastest for your audience.

## Descending acquisition priority

1. **Bootcamp:** strongest initial hypothesis for beginners who can attend live. Training reduces the setup objection; longer trial delays first billing. Main campaign.
2. **Pricing:** strongest purchase-intent destination. Clear plan choice suits a prospect who has already watched a demonstration. Test against bootcamp using equal-intent traffic.
3. **Main homepage:** flexible 14-day trial but broad messaging gives newcomers more decisions. Use after a focused tutorial.
4. **AI:** move to first place for a verified local-business audience with missed-call or appointment-handling needs. Demonstrate a narrow use case and explain the base subscription and separate AI/phone costs.
5. **Pro trial:** strongest base-plan earnings per customer; weaker general-audience affordability. Prioritize experienced agencies needing SaaS Mode.
6. **Annual:** useful for convinced, budget-qualified buyers. Larger upfront commitment can lower signups. Annual commissions are not a fresh monthly payment from the same customer.
7. **Affiliate upgrade:** an expansion route for eligible existing users, not a new-customer acquisition funnel. Could be quickest to incremental billing with an existing customer list. Split attribution matters.
8. **AI business workshop:** hold promotion until the exact supplied URL, next live dates and enrollment status are confirmed. Search found a differently spelled official route showing September 21–22, already past on the review date. Do not silently replace the supplied affiliate URL.

Ranking is editorial, not measured. For time to first payment among new prospects ready to buy: annual purchase (if checkout bills immediately) can be first; 14-day pricing/home/AI/Pro offers next; 30-day bootcamp after those; workshop unrankable until verified. Upgrades are a separate existing-customer category. None of this removes commission qualification and payout delays.

## Promotion and sequence

Choose one niche first: freelancers and small agencies setting up follow-up for local service businesses. Publish two useful YouTube demonstrations weekly and three short excerpts for LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook. Show one complete workflow, prerequisites and costs. Disclose commission verbally and visually near the beginning and in the first caption lines. Answer relevant community questions where promotion is allowed. Ask permission before sending links privately; never mass-message group members. Link each piece to a matching page and preserve the affiliate destination unchanged.

Days 1–14: verify portal, payout setup, brand/contact details, checkout and course schedule; record actual product walkthroughs; interview ten willing prospects; publish the page and first four videos. Days 15–45: obtain first attributable trials through existing consented relationships and useful content. Direct trial users to official onboarding. Track day-3 activation and first paid invoices. Days 46–90: compare bootcamp against pricing for the same audience, using paid retention and cost per paying account rather than cheap leads. Do not call a small sample a proven winner.

Start paid testing only after organic visitors produce attributable paying customers. With a $300 test budget, focus on one channel rather than splitting across platforms. Use generic Google Search phrases such as CRM setup training or follow-up automation for agencies, exact/phrase targeting and negative keywords for jobs, login and free downloads. Send to your informative independent page. Meta is an alternative for demonstrated video engagement, with consent before retargeting. LinkedIn is initially organic because a small budget may not support enough paid observations. No ad campaign has been launched.

Scale only when observed retained commission supports customer acquisition cost, content effort and churn. Reinvest a bounded portion of actually received commission. Review cohorts monthly. Target-fit upgrades can improve revenue after customers have real needs; never push an unnecessary plan or ask customers to cancel to change attribution. Retain through accurate expectation-setting and official support handoffs, not unauthorized signup bonuses.

## Measurement

Weekly: qualified landing visits; affiliate outbound clicks; portal trials; first paid accounts; retained paid accounts at days 60/90; refunds; plan mix; approved commissions; paid-out cash; ad spend. Trial conversion = trials / qualified visits. Paid conversion = first paid accounts / trial cohort. CAC = acquisition spend / new paid accounts, with organic labor reported separately. Use aggregate portal reconciliation or authorized postbacks; do not expose secrets in a static form or assume a CTA click proves a sale.

For an illustrative mix of 50% Starter, 35% Unlimited and 15% Pro, average monthly commission is $90.80. About 56 active accounts pass $5,000 gross; 100 imply $9,080 gross. With $1,000 ads and $200 tools, about 69 accounts are needed for $5,000 contribution before labor/tax. At 5% monthly churn, 100 active accounts require roughly five replacements monthly merely to stand still. The workbook makes traffic, conversion, churn, mix and costs editable and projects both the target date and approximate payout lag.
